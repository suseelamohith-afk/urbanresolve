Our Mission: Empowering Citizens, Resolving Issues

UResolve is a civic engagement platform designed to bridge the gap between citizens and local municipal bodies. Our goal is to create a transparent, efficient, and community-driven process for identifying, reporting, and resolving waste and civic issues, starting with Bengaluru.

How We Work

We leverage modern technology to streamline the often frustrating process of civic reporting:

Transparency and Accountability

Every report submitted is tracked in real-time, providing citizens with instant updates on the status, from assignment to resolution. This level of transparency ensures accountability from all stakeholders, including Municipal staff and dedicated field workers.

Data-Driven Action

The aggregated data from citizen reports helps Municipal staff identify hotspots, track category trends, and allocate resources efficiently. This leads to proactive, data-informed decision-making rather than reactive problem-solving.

Community Collaboration

UResolve thrives on community participation. By simplifying the reporting process to a few clicks, we encourage more citizens to become active monitors of their neighborhoods, fostering a shared responsibility for a cleaner city.

Our Partners

We are built in close collaboration with the Municipal Corporation to ensure seamless integration into existing civic resolution frameworks.
